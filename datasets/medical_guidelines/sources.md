## Source Selection Rules

Reference material should:

* Come from an authoritative medical organization or institution.
* Have a clearly identifiable source.
* Have publication or update information where available.
* Have usage rights appropriate for the project.
* Be relevant to the capabilities being tested.
* Not contain patient-identifiable information.

The AI system should use retrieved references as supporting evidence,
not as a replacement for qualified clinical judgment.
